Real-time Video Calling App

This demo uses WebRTC to share video and audio between two browser peers. Use one browser window as Caller and another as Receiver, then copy/paste signals.

Local Video
Remote Video
Controls
Local Signal (copy to remote peer)
Remote Signal (paste from other peer)
After starting the camera, generate an offer in one window and paste it into the other window's remote signal field. Then create an answer from the other window and paste it back to the first window. This manual signaling flow allows WebRTC to establish the peer connection.
